$dist(C_1, C_2): \sqrt{m + 4n}$

$$3 = \sqrt{m+4n}$$
$$9 = m + 4n$$
$$m = 9 - 4n$$
Then:
$$\frac{1}{n} + \frac{1}{m} = \frac{1}{n} + \frac{1}{9-4n}$$
