## 12. Domain and Range of Trigonometric Functions

> [!Note] Sine Function  
> The sine function is
> $$
> y=\sin x.
> $$
> 
> **Domain:**
> $$
> \mathrm{Dom}(\sin x)=\mathbb{R}
> $$
> 
> **Range:**
> $$
> \mathrm{Ran}(\sin x)=[-1,1].
> $$

> [!Note] Problem-Solving Insights — Sine  
> - $\sin x$ is defined for all real numbers.  
> - The output never exceeds $1$ in absolute value.

---

> [!Note] Cosine Function  
> The cosine function is
> $$
> y=\cos x.
> $$
> 
> **Domain:**
> $$
> \mathrm{Dom}(\cos x)=\mathbb{R}
> $$
> 
> **Range:**
> $$
> \mathrm{Ran}(\cos x)=[-1,1].
> $$

> [!Note] Problem-Solving Insights — Cosine  
> - Same domain and range as sine.  
> - Phase-shifted version of $\sin x$.

---

> [!Note] Tangent Function  
> The tangent function is
> $$
> y=\tan x=\frac{\sin x}{\cos x}.
> $$
> 
> **Domain:**
> $$
> \mathrm{Dom}(\tan x)=\mathbb{R}\setminus\left\{\frac{\pi}{2}+k\pi \mid k\in\mathbb{Z}\right\}
> $$
> 
> **Range:**
> $$
> \mathrm{Ran}(\tan x)=\mathbb{R}.
> $$

> [!Note] Problem-Solving Insights — Tangent  
> - Undefined where $\cos x=0$.  
> - Vertical asymptotes at $x=\frac{\pi}{2}+k\pi$.

---

> [!Note] Cotangent Function  
> The cotangent function is
> $$
> y=\cot x=\frac{\cos x}{\sin x}.
> $$
> 
> **Domain:**
> $$
> \mathrm{Dom}(\cot x)=\mathbb{R}\setminus\{k\pi \mid k\in\mathbb{Z}\}
> $$
> 
> **Range:**
> $$
> \mathrm{Ran}(\cot x)=\mathbb{R}.
> $$

> [!Note] Problem-Solving Insights — Cotangent  
> - Undefined where $\sin x=0$.  
> - Vertical asymptotes at integer multiples of $\pi$.

---

> [!Note] Secant Function  
> The secant function is
> $$
> y=\sec x=\frac{1}{\cos x}.
> $$
> 
> **Domain:**
> $$
> \mathrm{Dom}(\sec x)=\mathbb{R}\setminus\left\{\frac{\pi}{2}+k\pi \mid k\in\mathbb{Z}\right\}
> $$
> 
> **Range:**
> $$
> \mathrm{Ran}(\sec x)=(-\infty,-1]\cup[1,+\infty).
> $$

> [!Note] Problem-Solving Insights — Secant  
> - Reciprocal of cosine.  
> - Values cannot lie between $-1$ and $1$.

---

> [!Note] Cosecant Function  
> The cosecant function is
> $$
> y=\csc x=\frac{1}{\sin x}.
> $$
> 
> **Domain:**
> $$
> \mathrm{Dom}(\csc x)=\mathbb{R}\setminus\{k\pi \mid k\in\mathbb{Z}\}
> $$
> 
> **Range:**
> $$
> \mathrm{Ran}(\csc x)=(-\infty,-1]\cup[1,+\infty).
> $$

> [!Note] Problem-Solving Insights — Cosecant  
> - Reciprocal of sine.  
> - Same range structure as secant.

---

> [!Note] Summary Table — Trigonometric Functions

| Function | Domain | Range |
|--------|--------|-------|
| $\sin x$ | $\mathbb{R}$ | $[-1,1]$ |
| $\cos x$ | $\mathbb{R}$ | $[-1,1]$ |
| $\tan x$ | $\mathbb{R}\setminus\{\frac{\pi}{2}+k\pi\}$ | $\mathbb{R}$ |
| $\cot x$ | $\mathbb{R}\setminus\{k\pi\}$ | $\mathbb{R}$ |
| $\sec x$ | $\mathbb{R}\setminus\{\frac{\pi}{2}+k\pi\}$ | $(-\infty,-1]\cup[1,\infty)$ |
| $\csc x$ | $\mathbb{R}\setminus\{k\pi\}$ | $(-\infty,-1]\cup[1,\infty)$ |

---

> [!Note] General Problem-Solving Strategy  
> 1. Rewrite the function using $\sin x$ and $\cos x$.  
> 2. Exclude points where denominators are zero.  
> 3. Use known bounds of $\sin x$ and $\cos x$ to determine the range.

> [!Note] Additional Problems — Trigonometric Domain & Range  
> 4. Find the domain and range of $y=2\sin x-1$.  
> 5. Determine the domain of $y=\tan(2x+\frac{\pi}{3})$.  
> 6. Find the range of $y=1+\sec x$.
