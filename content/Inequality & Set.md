## Set Theory

> [!Note] Set  
> A **set** is a collection of distinct objects, called **elements**.
> 
> Common notations:
> - $x\in A$: $x$ is an element of set $A$  
> - $x\notin A$: $x$ is not an element of set $A$
> 
> Sets can be described by:
> - **Listing elements:** $A=\{1,2,3\}$  
> - **Property notation:**  
>   $$
>   A=\{x\in\mathbb{R}\mid x>0\}
>   $$

---

> [!Note] Special Sets  
> - **Empty set:** $\varnothing$  
> - **Universal set:** $U$ (depends on context)  
> - **Natural numbers:** $\mathbb{N}$  
> - **Integers:** $\mathbb{Z}$  
> - **Rational numbers:** $\mathbb{Q}$  
> - **Real numbers:** $\mathbb{R}$

---

> [!Note] Subset  
> A set $A$ is a **subset** of $B$, written $A\subseteq B$, if
> $$
> \forall x,\ x\in A \Rightarrow x\in B.
> $$

A = {2, 3, 4}, 

> [!Note] Proper Subset  
> $A$ is a **proper subset** of $B$, written $A\subset B$, if $A\subseteq B$ and $A\ne B$.

---

> [!Note] Set Operations  
> Let $A,B$ be sets.
> 
> - **Union:**  
>   $$
>   A\cup B=\{x\mid x\in A \text{ or } x\in B\}
>   $$
> - **Intersection:**  
>   $$
>   A\cap B=\{x\mid x\in A \text{ and } x\in B\}
>   $$
> - **Difference:**  
>   $$
>   A\setminus B=\{x\mid x\in A \text{ and } x\notin B\}
>   $$
> - **Complement:**  
>   $$
>   A^c=\{x\in U\mid x\notin A\}
>   $$

---

> [!Note] Properties of Set Operations  
> - $A\cup B=B\cup A$  
> - $A\cap B=B\cap A$  
> - $A\cup(A\cap B)=A$  
> - $A\cap(A\cup B)=A$  
> - $(A\cup B)^c=A^c\cap B^c$  
> - $(A\cap B)^c=A^c\cup B^c$

---

> [!Note] Problem-Solving Insights — Sets  
> - Draw **Venn diagrams** to visualize relationships.  
> - Translate word problems into set notation first.  
> - Always identify the **universal set**.

---

> [!Note] Additional Problems — Set Theory  
> 1. If $A=\{1,2,3\}$ and $B=\{3,4,5\}$, find $A\cup B$ and $A\cap B$.  
> 2. Let $U=\{1,2,3,4,5\}$ and $A=\{2,4\}$. Find $A^c$.  
> 3. Prove $A\cap\varnothing=\varnothing$.  
> 4. Show that $A\subseteq B$ if and only if $A\cap B=A$.

## Inequalities 

> [!Note] Inequality  
> An **inequality** is a mathematical statement comparing two expressions using
> $$
> <,\ \le,\ >,\ \ge.
> $$

---

> [!Note] Basic Properties of Inequalities  
> For real numbers $a,b,c$:
> - If $a<b$, then $a+c<b+c$  
> - If $a<b$ and $c>0$, then $ac<bc$  
> - If $a<b$ and $c<0$, then $ac>bc$ (inequality reverses)

---

> [!Note] Solving Linear Inequalities  
> To solve an inequality:
> 1. Simplify both sides  
> 2. Isolate the variable  
> 3. Reverse the inequality sign if multiplying or dividing by a negative number

> [!Note] Example  
> Solve
> $$
> 2x-3<5 \Rightarrow x<4.
> $$

---

> [!Note] Compound Inequalities  
> A **compound inequality** has the form
> $$
> a<x\le b.
> $$

> [!Note] Example  
> $$
> -1\le2x+1<5 \Rightarrow -1\le x<2.
> $$

---

> [!Note] Quadratic Inequalities  
> A **quadratic inequality** has the form
> $$
> ax^2+bx+c\ge0.
> $$

> [!Note] Method — Quadratic Inequalities  
> 1. Factor the quadratic (if possible)  
> 2. Find zeros  
> 3. Use a sign chart  
> 4. Choose intervals satisfying the inequality

---

> [!Note] Absolute Value Inequalities  
> For $a>0$:
> - $|x|<a \iff -a<x<a$  
> - $|x|>a \iff x<-a \text{ or } x>a$

---

> [!Note] Problem-Solving Insights — Inequalities  
> - Always check boundary points.  
> - Express solutions using **interval notation**.  
> - Use number lines to visualize solutions.

---

> [!Note] Additional Problems — Inequalities  
> 1. Solve $3x-7\ge2$.  
> 2. Solve $x^2-5x\le0$.  
> 3. Solve $|x-2|<3$.  
> 4. Write the solution of $x>1$ in interval notation.
